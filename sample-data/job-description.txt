Senior Frontend Engineer

We're a mid-size fintech company building tools for small business owners.
We're looking for a Senior Frontend Engineer to help lead our web platform.

About the role:
You'll own significant parts of our customer-facing dashboard, mentor
junior engineers, and work closely with design and product to ship
features end to end.

Requirements:
- 4+ years of professional experience building production web applications
- Strong proficiency in React, TypeScript, and CSS
- Experience with REST APIs and Git-based workflows
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field (or equivalent
  practical experience)
- Comfortable working in an agile, fast-paced environment

Nice to have:
- Experience with GraphQL
- Familiarity with Next.js
- Docker / containerized deployments
- Prior experience in fintech or another regulated industry

What we offer:
Competitive salary, equity, remote-friendly culture, and a team that cares
about shipping thoughtful, well-tested software.
